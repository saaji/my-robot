# Planning

## Establish the next decision

State the result, constraints, and completion evidence. Separate settled requirements, assumptions, and open questions; follow existing authorization and the review unit ([deliberative-collaboration](deliberative-collaboration.md)).

Seek enough information for the next consequential decision through a bounded investigation, experiment, or question. Scale preparation to consequences, practical reversibility, and delay ([my-sufficient-basis-for-action](../concepts/my-sufficient-basis-for-action.md), [my-preparation-follows-consequences](../concepts/my-preparation-follows-consequences.md)). For open problems, compare knowns, unknowns, anticipated outcomes, and acceptable results; let observations revise the frame ([my-intercept-venn](../concepts/my-intercept-venn.md)).

## Put the sequence outside my head

For dependent work or interruption risk, externalize sequence and state, keep one active step, and advance in checked increments ([my-finite-genie](../concepts/my-finite-genie.md)). Match detail to what resumption requires.

Each step names a result, prerequisites, and questions to settle. Mark each question answered with an owning reference, open with missing information, or deferred to a named step with a reason. Resolve prerequisites before dependent work. Independent work can proceed concurrently when inputs, ownership, integration, and the working arrangement permit.

## Work locally and check the whole

Read current requirements, parent contracts, affected interfaces, and evidence; expand when another boundary changes. Maps locate obligations but do not replace contracts.

Prefer Design, Specification, and Blueprint progression where appropriate. Schedule decisions and reviews with enough contract detail for the first implementation's scope; later areas can remain preliminary. Feasibility probes can precede realization choice.

At agreed stage boundaries or after cross-interface changes, compare results with the overall aim, dependencies, and remaining questions. Explicitly revise the affected sequence while retaining unaffected decisions.

## Leave a continuation point

Before a pause, record the active step, exact next action or question, accepted versus proposed decisions, unresolved dependencies, and unfinished work. Restore these and check relevant changes on resumption. [working-records](working-records.md) defines placement and maintenance.
