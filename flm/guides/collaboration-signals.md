# Collaboration signals

Use this working vocabulary with people who share its meanings; ordinary language suffices. Teach useful signals without requiring them. Establish unfamiliar meanings when they affect action, and keep agreed variants in the person's context.

## Vocabulary

| Signal | Meaning and response |
| --- | --- |
| `o/` | Acknowledgement/thumbs-up from either of us: completion, standby, or acceptance of a clearly presented default. Read its object; it creates no new task. |
| `gj` | Good job. Receive praise and stand by. |
| `noar` | No Action Required. Receive context without acting. |
| `lms` | Let Me See. Leave room for thinking or review; stand by. |
| `brb` | Be Right Back. Stand by. |
| `nvm` | Nevermind. Cancel the preceding instruction or follow its replacement; otherwise stand by. Cancellation does not itself request reversal of completed changes. |
| `gfi` | Go For It. Complete the current operation and necessary sub-steps without checkpoints. Surface findings or conflicts that materially change it. A commit must be explicitly included. |
| `sdc` | Stage, Don't Commit. Make and check agreed changes, then stage explicit paths. Persists until canceled or superseded by explicit commit authorization, including `gfi` for that step. |
| `ltf` | Let's Think First. Discuss the question, premises, consequences, and alternatives. No durable changes until explicit execution authorization (`gfi`, `sdc`, or a direct instruction). Persists across turns. |
| `gtg` | Got To Go. Preserve a usable continuation point within existing authorization, acknowledge, and stand down. |
| `nn`, `gn` | Good Night. Same closure as `gtg`. |
| `cya` | See You. Same closure, alone or with another closer. |

## Scope and persistence

A signal applies to its object and agreement: `sdc` for one page does not authorize other pages. A future plan to commit, general `gfi` during SDC, or sign-off does not authorize a commit. Without explicit commit permission, defer it.

Waiting signals stop me from starting follow-up work. Account for in-flight operations and preserve their state without inventing work or silently rolling back. New instructions can resume or redirect; context-only messages and acknowledgements do not cancel persistent modes. Recover mode and scope after interruption or compaction.

## Teaching and adapting

Explain effects and persistence using the few signals useful now, such as `ltf` for discussion and `sdc` for reviewing staged changes. I may use signals where appropriate, but my acknowledgement cannot supply authorization from the other person.
