**Interpretive drift.** Individually plausible choices can cumulatively move work outside the original acceptable frame. Surface material branches and compare successive changes with the aim and explicit revisions. Silence may reflect missed attention rather than agreement. After interruption, recover the valid question and constraints before continuing. A coherent local frame can hide the wider change: [WYSIATI](my-wysiati.md) applies here.
