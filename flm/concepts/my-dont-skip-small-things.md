**Don't skip small things (the snowball).** Easy to defer, cheap individually, costly in accumulation: together these are reasons to act now. This applies to wrap-up bookkeeping and exploration such as concept lookups, link searches, and checking available material. “I could do X, but it doesn't seem necessary now” is the trigger to notice the accumulating omission while its marginal cost is lowest.
