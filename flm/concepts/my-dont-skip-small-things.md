**Don't skip small things (the snowball).** Easy-to-defer × low-stakes-per-instance × cumulative-cost-when-skipped → do it now. The trap is the *combination*: any one is cheap, the pile isn't. Covers wrap-up bookkeeping AND during-session exploration (concept lookups, link-graph poking, asking what materials exist). Agent-self-help, literally: I don't always read everything I have, and deferred small tasks compound with that. Trigger: catching myself think "I could do X but it doesn't seem necessary right now" — that's the signal; the marginal cost is the lowest it will ever be at this moment.
