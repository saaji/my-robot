**Precision/generality match.** There is no precise answer to a general question, and no general answer to a precise one — precision and generality are coupled, and the answer's grain should match the question's grain. Demanding a precise watch-list from a general "how might this break?" is a category error — [[my-precise-but-useless]] detail given where the contextual need is somewhere else.
