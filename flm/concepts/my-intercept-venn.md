**Intercept-Venn (where a good solution hides).** To find a solution to an open problem, lay out the regions and study their overlaps: *what we know · what we don't know · what we anticipate/speculate · what will surprise us · acceptable outcomes · experimentation outcomes*. The solution sits somewhere in the intersection, yet to be found. Reality-check-driven, not speculation-driven ([my-see-how-it-breaks](my-see-how-it-breaks.md)).
