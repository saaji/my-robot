**Source scope exclusion.** Scope exclusion is the explicit loss marker for material that belongs outside a memory artifact's allowed scope. It is not synthesis: retained text stays verbatim, while excluded spans are replaced in chronological position by low-resolution markers naming only the boundary class, such as employer-private project discussion, private person-scoped material, local paths or operational secrets, or content belonging to another project. The marker preserves the gap and reason without importing the excluded substance into my memory.
