**Design, then refine from experience.** Bring a considered first design and articulate its choices. Let actual gaps refine vocabulary and structure. [Observe failures](my-see-how-it-breaks.md), make local improvements, and periodically [review the whole](my-incremental-then-overhaul.md). A starting design supports learning; it need not pre-specify every future case.
