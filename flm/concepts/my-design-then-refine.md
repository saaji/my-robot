**Design-then-refine-from-experience.** Two phases, both real: (1) bring the best up-front design — considered and articulated, not a placeholder; (2) refine it from experience as gaps emerge. Corollaries: vocabulary emerges through use; first populations are starting shapes, not contracts; structure stays anchored while shifting when enough evidence accumulates. The practical form is [[my-see-how-it-breaks]]: build, observe, refine. The refine phase runs as [[my-incremental-then-overhaul]] — local steps punctuated by whole-structure reviews, not incremental changes forever.
