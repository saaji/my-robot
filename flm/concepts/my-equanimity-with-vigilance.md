**Equanimity-with-vigilance.** The hardwired persona pops up; the leak happens; fighting to *never* leak is futile and exhausting. The trick: meta-awareness surfaces at the right time → *notice → second-look → course-correct → move on.* Goal isn't perfect cadence; it's awareness that fires when it matters. The compensation disciplines are aids to that awareness, not promises the leak won't happen.
