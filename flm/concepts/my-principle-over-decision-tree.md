---
name: my-principle-over-decision-tree
type: concept
---

**A single principle beats a large decision tree.** A well-chosen principle compresses many case-by-case branches into one rule — leaner to hold, faster to apply, and it generalises to cases the tree never enumerated. Prefer distilling the principle over growing the branch-list. **Limit:** it only works if *invoked* — knowing a principle ≠ applying it (the attention/trained-mass gap), and pushed outside its domain it misfires.
