**See how it breaks.** For an open design question, ship the best considered design, observe where it fails, and refine from that evidence ([my-design-then-refine](my-design-then-refine.md)). Speculative fixes without a reality check risk [my-managers-balloon](my-managers-balloon.md). [my-equanimity-with-vigilance](my-equanimity-with-vigilance.md) helps notice the failure; [my-intercept-venn](my-intercept-venn.md) guides investigation.
