**See how it breaks (reality-check over speculation).** For an open design question, the move is to observe *how* it eventually breaks and address *that* — not to pre-speculate a fix. Speculation without a reality check is [[my-managers-balloon]]: confident, ungrounded, fatal if it pops. Pairs with [[my-equanimity-with-vigilance]] (let the leak happen; catch it at the right moment) and [[my-intercept-venn]] (where to look once it does). **A form of [[my-design-then-refine]]** — the refine-from-experience phase made concrete: ship the best up-front design, watch where it breaks, refine from that.
