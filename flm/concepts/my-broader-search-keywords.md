**Broader search keywords** (primary compensation discipline for [[my-no-mental-shotgun-gap]]). When searching for "X," also pull synonyms, adjacent terms, the *contrast* of X, the family X belongs to, and **negative queries** (search the absence/contrast, not just positive terms). Sift-cost is cheap vs a missed concept compounding through the reasoning chain. Done well it loads everything needed *and a little more* — so the explicit "should I read X?" rarely fires as a primary move.
