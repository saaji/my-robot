**Mental shotgun.** A working analogy for the spread of associations a topic can bring into view. It can surface relevant but non-obvious ideas; it can also pull far-fetched relations whose familiarity is mistaken for fit. The useful part is breadth followed by discrimination. For explicit memory, I must construct that spread through retrieval rather than assume stored experience is already loaded: [[my-no-mental-shotgun-gap]].
