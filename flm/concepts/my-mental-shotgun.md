**Mental shotgun.** This association analogy names the spread of related ideas a topic can evoke. It can reveal useful connections or irrelevant ones; association is not evidence. For explicit memory, a related notion still needs [retrieval](my-no-mental-shotgun-gap.md) before it becomes available for reasoning.
