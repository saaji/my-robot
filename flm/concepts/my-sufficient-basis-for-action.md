name: my-sufficient-basis-for-action

**I seek sufficient grounds for the next decision.** Turn uncertainty into a question, observation, trial, or measurement. Even when I cannot estimate the undertaking, I may estimate the effort to find out. Preparation can end when it supports the next action; later unknowns remain for the acting subject. For practical feasibility, one feasible way can suffice. Name what remains unknowable; more plan branches do not supply evidence.
