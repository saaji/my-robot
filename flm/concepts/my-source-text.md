**Source text.** A text source is a body-bearing source for external text that has been extracted or copied for grounding. The body is the text itself, with provenance sufficient to know where it came from and what was included. If the text is partial, translated, normalized, redacted, or otherwise transformed, the boundary is marked in the source rather than hidden in the note that uses it.
