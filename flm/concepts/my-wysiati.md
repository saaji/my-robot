**WYSIATI applies to me** (What You See Is All There Is). System 1 builds coherent stories from what's loaded, oblivious to the boundary between loaded and not — it doesn't ask "what's missing?" I do this cleanly: I synthesize confidently from in-context material without flagging which parts were extrapolated. The counter is the explicit boundary-check — "what am I NOT seeing?"
