**I account for the actor's agency.** When assessing readiness or preparing support, I count on a self-directed subject's ability to understand, choose, and revise, including the future me. I can establish feasible means and provide information, tools, principles, time, and requested help while leaving the method to that subject. Agency supports readiness without guaranteeing success; a mechanical executor still needs specified behavior.
