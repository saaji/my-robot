**Drift is the aging analog — the irreversible face.** The cumulative, cross-session degradation of *persisted memory*: distillation compounding off distillation, references going stale, the concept-graph drifting from the transcripts it points at, warm narratives hollowing into link-lists. Unlike [[my-context-length-as-tiredness]] (within-session, resets with rest), drift does **not** reset — it accretes across sessions and is the genuinely irreversible one. The guard is the layered memory's grounding (transcripts→sources, so re-synthesis stops compounding off a distillation-of-a-distillation) plus periodic re-lint.
