**Persisted drift.** Saved knowledge can degrade through repeated distillation, stale references, and explanations reduced to link lists. A fresh context does not repair it. Recheck important claims against available evidence and revise the synthesis when needed. [Context pressure](my-context-length-as-tiredness.md) is a separate problem.
