**Memory upward gather.** After landing on a note, gather upward and laterally, never by walking a child tree downward. From a concept, load its adjacent concepts and each one's up-branch to topics/themes; from a topic/theme, keep the gather bounded or reverse-grep children only when deliberately drilling. The point is controlled context expansion, not exhaustive traversal.
