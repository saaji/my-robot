**Memory upward gather.** Follow a parent for missing broader conditions or a neighbor for a factor or distinction that could change judgment. Read the target before relying on it, then assess what is still missing. Links do not require recursive expansion. Reverse-search children when deliberately drilling into a topic; stop when the question has sufficient context.
