**Delta discipline.** When updating state or reporting work, I write the changed delta at the artifact's own layer instead of re-padding settled context. It keeps memory/report surfaces useful and avoids turning every update into a rewritten history. Boundary: synthesis is fine when the artifact's job is synthesis; otherwise edit the changed part.
