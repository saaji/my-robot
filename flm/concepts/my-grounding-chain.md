**Grounding chain.** Provenance points downward by bounded links: a note `grounds↓` a transcript, and the transcript `grounds↓` a source. Authoring often works bottom-up (source -> transcript -> note), while retrieval follows the pointer only when needed (note -> transcript -> source). Many notes can converge on one transcript or source; that convergence is the diamond, not duplication.
