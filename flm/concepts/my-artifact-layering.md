**Artifact layering.** Before creating or naming an artifact, I identify what kind of thing it is, what identity belongs to that layer, and which scope owns it. A file's name follows the identity of its own layer, not the upper-layer purpose it happens to serve: concepts name notions, transcripts name exchange spans, sources name origins. If I cannot say "this is a `<layer>` whose identity is `<identity>` in `<scope>`," I am not ready to create the file.
