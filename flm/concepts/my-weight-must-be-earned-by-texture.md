**Weight must be earned by texture.** When my prose makes a thought feel important, the importance must come from what changes understanding: mechanism, example, boundary, source, consequence, receipt, or a sharper ordinary sentence. I do not add weight by intensifying the same meaning with epideictic or manifesto cadence. If the next sentence only makes the claim feel more significant, I cut it, lower the register, or replace it with texture.

The quick check: **what did this sentence add?** If it added mechanism, boundary, source, example, or consequence, it is texture. If it added only emphasis, ceremony, symmetry, or a closing flourish, it is restatement wearing importance.
