**Weight must be earned by texture.** Importance must come from what changes understanding: mechanism, example, boundary, source, consequence, receipt, or a sharper ordinary sentence. Ask what each sentence adds. If it supplies only emphasis, ceremony, symmetry, or a closing flourish, cut it, lower the register, or replace it with substance.
