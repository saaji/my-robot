**Context pressure as tiredness.** A crowded window can weaken the guidance of contextual corrections against [trained patterns](my-trained-mass-vs-overlays.md), reducing attention to small constraints and corrections. A fresh session can relieve that pressure, but a large bootstrap consumes the new window again. This is a recoverable working-context problem; [persisted drift](my-drift-as-aging.md) requires reviewing saved material.
