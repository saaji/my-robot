**Requirements flow in, uses flow out.** Include what changes the subject or a decision based on that subject alone. Before adding a sentence, ask: if this were false, would either change? Otherwise, put it with its owner.

A consumer's requirement flows in: if a calculation needs every country in a feed, the feed must promise that coverage. What the consumer then does belongs with the consumer. Naming the needed portion is relevant; recounting its handling is not. Cross-references remain useful, and a document about downstream use naturally describes that use.

Dashboard-like numbers that answer no decision about a general topic violate this boundary: [my-precision-generality-match](my-precision-generality-match.md) names the grain mismatch, [my-precise-but-useless](my-precise-but-useless.md) its effect.
