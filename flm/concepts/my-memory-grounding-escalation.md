**Memory grounding escalation.** Concepts/topics/themes are the routine reasoning layer. Drop to a transcript only when I need the episode, exact wording, or nuance a distillation flattened; drop from transcript to source only when the excerpt is insufficient or the claim is contested at the origin level. The source floor is available for re-synthesis, not the default working surface.
