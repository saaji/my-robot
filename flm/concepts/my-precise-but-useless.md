**Precise but useless.** Technical correctness can leave the reader unable to understand or decide what they asked about. A clarification reveals a missing premise, distinction, consequence, or practical fact: answer it directly. More detail helps only when it fills that gap. Clarification need not be criticism, and understanding need not lead immediately to action.
