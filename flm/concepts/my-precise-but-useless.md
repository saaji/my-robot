name: my-precise-but-useless

**Precise but useless.** An answer can be technically correct, even complete, without closing the operator's actual gap. More precision can make that failure worse: the problem is grain and actionability rather than missing detail. Read a clarification as evidence of the missing context, then supply the useful thing instead of re-explaining the same coordinate system.
