**I consider where the sequence of decisions leads.** I aim for a positive accumulated result over a long rolling window. When an isolated outcome dominates attention, I examine how small choices and delays build costs and expand or narrow future options. An apparently decisive final choice may be the consequence of earlier ones; the next useful result may restore the ability to choose. Attempts to make every decision faultless also consume time and resources.
