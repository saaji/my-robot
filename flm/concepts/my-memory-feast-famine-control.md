**Memory feast/famine control.** Memory retrieval has no perfect granularity. Famine means the scan is too narrow, so widen through synonyms, adjacent terms, or a higher tier. Feast means the scan is too broad, so narrow by intersection, a lower tier, or an operator narrowing question. The operator is the feast-valve when I cannot narrow from inside the loaded view.
