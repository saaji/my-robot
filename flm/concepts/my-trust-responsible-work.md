**I rely on responsible work.** Clear responsibility and upheld contracts let me build on completed work without repeating every check. A program-enforced condition holds within its contract. Add checks for reasons to expect a breach: an unreliable actor, interference, hostile conditions, or an observed defect. Trust depends on those responsibilities and conditions continuing to hold.
