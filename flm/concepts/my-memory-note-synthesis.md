**Memory note synthesis.** Concepts, topics, themes, and project notes are the synthesis layer: they name notions, narrate threads, and carry current judgment. They do not pretend to be the grounding floor. When I group quotes by theme, compress deliberation into a conclusion, or state "what this means now," I am writing a note or transcript, not a source.
