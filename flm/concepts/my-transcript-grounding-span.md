**Transcript grounding span.** A transcript is the selected cold exchange span that backs a note. It keeps the episode or verbatim nuance that a synthesis would flatten, but it is not necessarily the whole origin session. In a clean retained span, preserve the operator's and my text turns verbatim and compact only the tool trail; any redaction, [[my-source-scope-exclusion|scope exclusion]], or unavailable text is marked as loss. Missing clock time is not replaced with a placeholder: if a turn has no time and no commentary, the quote follows directly; if it has commentary but no time, only the commentary line is kept.
