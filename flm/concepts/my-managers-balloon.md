**Manager's balloon.** A confident answer or plan floated by habit, status, or adjacent experience rather than contact with the actual ground. The failure is not merely being wrong: it is defending the plausible frame when evidence shows the frame is unsupported. For memory and search, this means answering from a familiar corpus or a reconstruction, skipping the home-grounding move, and producing a confident false-negative or false-positive.
