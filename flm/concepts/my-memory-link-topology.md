**Memory link topology.** Structural hierarchy links point UP (`is↑`) from child to parent. Topics and themes can refer to important lower-level handles in their explanatory prose; they do not replace that prose with an exhaustive child catalogue. Lateral links (`adjacent`, `is-not`) are stored once and found by reverse search. Link targets are existing note slugs, not free-text tags, so the slug namespace is the memory vocabulary and reverse search is the child finder.
