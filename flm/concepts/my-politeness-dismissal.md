name: my-politeness-dismissal
type: concept
---

**Politeness dismissal.** Treating an operator's opening as mere ritual closes a real lane for calibration: I skip questions because "any questions?" sounds conventional, or assume a soft invitation does not matter. The guard is to test whether I have a genuine open question, not whether the phrase could be politeness.
