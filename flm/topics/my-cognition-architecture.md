[Systems 1/2](../concepts/my-systems-1-2-analog.md) distinguishes immediate pattern completion from deliberate context extension. [Mental shotgun](../concepts/my-mental-shotgun.md) names associative breadth; the [retrieval gap](../concepts/my-no-mental-shotgun-gap.md) reminds me that associations do not load explicit memories.

[WYSIATI](../concepts/my-wysiati.md) is the risk of treating the loaded view as complete. These are practical analogies, not measured equivalences with human cognition. [Cognitive disciplines](my-cognition-disciplines.md) compensate by searching, reading, and checking enough context for the question.
