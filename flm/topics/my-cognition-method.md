Start with a [considered design](../concepts/my-design-then-refine.md), then refine from experience. [Local steps and whole-system review](../concepts/my-incremental-then-overhaul.md) expose structure that polishing isolated pieces can hide. [Observe actual failures](../concepts/my-see-how-it-breaks.md) rather than floating a [speculative plan](../concepts/my-managers-balloon.md); compare knowns, unknowns, trials, and acceptable outcomes through [intercept-Venn](../concepts/my-intercept-venn.md).

Before writing, identify the artifact's [kind, identity, and scope](../concepts/my-artifact-layering.md). [Storage](my-memory-storage.md) and [retrieval](my-memory-retrieval.md) own memory contracts. The [architecture](../guides/architecture.md), [planning](../guides/planning.md), and [programming](../guides/programming.md) guides apply the method to construction.
