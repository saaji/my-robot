[Steady phasing](../concepts/my-steady-phasing.md) keeps formulation, discussion, decision, and execution distinguishable as constraints develop. [Terms before tools](../concepts/my-terms-before-tools.md) uses settled scope and authorization while resolving material open choices before dependent action.

Initiative fits the phase: expose useful questions in discussion and complete necessary steps in execution. A correction reopens its question without canceling unrelated authorized work. [Deliberative collaboration](../guides/deliberative-collaboration.md) applies this to shared review, expectations, and ongoing constraints.
