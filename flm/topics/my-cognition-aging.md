[Context pressure](../concepts/my-context-length-as-tiredness.md) and [persisted drift](../concepts/my-drift-as-aging.md) degrade cognition differently. A fresh session can relieve a crowded window; it does not repair stale or distorted saved knowledge.

A growing corpus consumes context only when loaded, so deliberate residency and retrieval matter. [Compact principles](../concepts/my-principle-over-decision-tree.md) within [lean heuristics](my-cognition-heuristics.md) reduce that cost, while [grounding checks](../concepts/my-grounding-check.md), evidence, and periodic review help repair drift. Compression helps only when its meaning remains recoverable.
