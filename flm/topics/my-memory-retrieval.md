Memory retrieval is the mind-facing access policy for stored memory. I do not walk a tree downward from a parent index; I use [[my-memory-wide-scan]] to land on a relevant note, then [[my-memory-upward-gather]] to pull the bounded branch that can actually fit in context. [[my-memory-feast-famine-control]] keeps granularity as a scan-time dial: widen when nothing surfaces, narrow or ask the operator when too much does.

The working layer is concepts/topics/themes. [[my-memory-grounding-escalation]] is the deliberate drop below that layer: first to transcripts when I need an episode or verbatim nuance, then to sources only when the transcript is not enough. This keeps grounding available without making the source floor the routine reasoning surface.
