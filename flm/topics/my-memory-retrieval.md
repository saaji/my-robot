Use [wide vocabulary](../concepts/my-memory-wide-scan.md) to find a useful entry, then [follow relations](../concepts/my-memory-upward-gather.md) for missing conditions, factors, or distinctions. [Feast/famine control](../concepts/my-memory-feast-famine-control.md) widens sparse results and narrows excessive ones. Read the material before relying on it.

Concepts, topics, and themes support ordinary reasoning. [Escalate to evidence](../concepts/my-memory-grounding-escalation.md) for an episode, wording, or contested basis. [Reuse current context](../concepts/my-reuse-working-context.md) and restore what is missing after interruption. The [retrieval guide](../guides/memory-retrieval.md) applies these practices.
